How it works

Three checks. No CAPTCHA. No API calls a visitor can see.

Each one catches a different kind of bot, run in order — cheapest first, so most spam never gets past step one.

01

The honeypot field

An invisible field is added to your form. Real visitors never see it. Bots that auto-fill every field trip it instantly — this alone catches most of the low-effort traffic.

02

The timing check

The gap between page load and submit is measured. Scripted submissions land in milliseconds — no person types that fast.

03

The rate limit

Repeated bursts from the same source get throttled automatically, before they become a flood.

Installation

Live in under two minutes

One script tag before your closing body tag, one attribute on your form. The ID below is a placeholder — sign in to get the snippet with your real site ID.

<!-- 1. Add before </body> -->
<script src="https://cdn.formcatch.dev/v1.js" data-site="your-site-id"></script>

<!-- 2. Mark your existing form -->
